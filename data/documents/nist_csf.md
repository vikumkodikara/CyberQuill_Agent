# NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0

## Overview

The NIST Cybersecurity Framework provides a policy framework of computer security guidance for how organizations can assess and improve their ability to prevent, detect, and respond to cyber attacks. Version 2.0 adds the Govern function and emphasizes supply chain risk management and organizational cybersecurity strategy.

## Key Concepts & Attack Vectors

- **GOVERN (GV)**: Establishes cybersecurity risk management strategy, expectations, and policy with executive oversight.
- **IDENTIFY (ID)**: Develops organizational understanding of assets, business context, and cybersecurity risks.
- **PROTECT (PR)**: Implements safeguards including access control, awareness training, and data security.
- **DETECT (DE)**: Defines activities to identify cybersecurity events through continuous monitoring and anomaly detection.
- **RESPOND (RS)**: Takes action on detected incidents through response planning, analysis, and mitigation.
- **RECOVER (RC)**: Restores impaired capabilities and services through recovery planning and improvements.

## Detection & Indicators

- Measure maturity against CSF Implementation Tiers (Partial, Risk Informed, Repeatable, Adaptive).
- Track gap analysis between Current Profile and Target Profile for each CSF function.
- Monitor KPIs for detection time (MTTD) and response time (MTTR) aligned with Detect and Respond functions.
- Assess supply chain risk indicators as part of the Govern function requirements.
- Evaluate control effectiveness through regular internal audits mapped to CSF categories.

## Mitigation & Best Practices

- **Framework Profiles**: Create Current and Target Profiles to prioritize cybersecurity investments.
- **Risk Assessment**: Conduct regular risk assessments aligned with Identify function outcomes.
- **Continuous Monitoring**: Implement security monitoring solutions for the Detect function.
- **Incident Response Planning**: Maintain tested IR plans mapped to Respond and Recover functions.
- **Supply Chain Security**: Extend Govern function requirements to third-party vendor risk management.

## Incident Response Considerations

- Activate incident response plan aligned with Respond (RS) function categories.
- Execute containment and mitigation activities per predefined RS subcategories.
- Document lessons learned and update recovery plans per Recover (RC) function.
- Report incidents to leadership per Govern function communication requirements.
- Update organizational risk register and Target Profile based on incident findings.
