# Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI)

## 1. Overview & Objectives

**Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI)** is evidence-based knowledge—including context, mechanisms, indicators, implications, and actionable advice—about existing or emerging hazards to assets.

---

## 2. The Four Tiers of CTI

1. **Strategic Intelligence**: High-level analysis for executive decision-makers (geopolitical risks, threat actor motivation, budget planning).
2. **Operational Intelligence**: Insights into specific campaign tactics, capabilities, and upcoming threat actor operations.
3. **Tactical Intelligence**: Technical details on threat actor TTPs (Tactics, Techniques, and Procedures mapped to MITRE ATT&CK).
4. **Technical Intelligence**: Short-lived Indicators of Compromise (IOCs)—IP addresses, file hashes, malicious domains, and registry keys.

---

## 3. Threat Intelligence Standards & Sharing

- **STIX (Structured Threat Information eXpression)**: Standardized language for serializing threat intelligence data (JSON-based).
- **TAXII (Trusted Automated eXchange of Intelligence Information)**: Protocol defining RESTful services for exchanging STIX threat intelligence over HTTPS.
- **The Diamond Model**: Framework mapping intrusion events across four core nodes: *Adversary*, *Capability*, *Infrastructure*, and *Victim*.
